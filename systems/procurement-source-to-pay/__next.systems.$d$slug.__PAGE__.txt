## What this system is

Procurement (source-to-pay) is the **buying chain**: from finding a supplier, through contracting and ordering, to paying the invoice. The ERP owns the financial tail; procurement systems own the process spine. The goal is *controlled spend* — every purchase approved, captured, and matched before money moves.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  SR[Supplier and sourcing] --> CT[Contract]
  CT --> RQ[Purchase requisition]
  RQ --> PO[Purchase order]
  PO --> GR[Goods receipt]
  GR --> IV[Invoice]
  IV --> MT{3-way match}
  MT -->|match| PY[Payment]
  MT -->|mismatch| EX[Exception - hold]
```

## The core documents

| Document | Who creates it | What it captures | The control it provides |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Purchase requisition | Requester | What + why + budget | Approval routing before any commitment |
| Purchase order | Buyer | Supplier, items, price, terms | Legal commitment to buy |
| Goods receipt | Warehouse | What actually arrived | Physical truth of delivery |
| Invoice | Supplier / AP | What they want paid | The ask for money |

## The 3-way match

The 3-way match is the beating heart of source-to-pay: **PO price vs invoice price vs receipt quantity**. If all three agree, the invoice clears. If not, it sits on a hold until a person resolves it. This one control prevents most overpayment and duplicate-payment fraud — and it is exactly the behavior to verify in testing.

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
  participant Buyer
  participant AP as Accounts payable
  participant Sys as ERP/Procurement
  Buyer->>Sys: PO created and approved
  Sys->>Warehouse: receive goods, post receipt
  Warehouse->>Sys: goods receipt (qty + quality ok)
  Supplier->>AP: invoice submitted
  AP->>Sys: invoice → 3-way match
  alt PO = invoice = receipt
    Sys->>Pay: auto-clears for payment
  else price or qty mismatch
    Sys->>Hold: invoice on hold, workflow routed
  end
```

The failure modes to care about: **partial or over shipments** (receipt qty vs PO qty), **price drift** (invoice deviates from PO), and **duplicate invoices** (same invoice number posted twice across a supplier portal and EDI).

## The request → happy path

```mermaid
stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> Req: create
  Req: Requisition
  Req --> PO: approve + commit
  PO: Purchase order
  PO --> GR: receive
  GR: Goods receipt
  GR --> Inv: 3-way match
  Inv: Invoice
  Inv --> Pay: cleared
  Pay: Payment
  Pay --> [*]
```

## Tester checklist

- 3-way match: over-shipment, under-shipment, price drift, and duplicate invoice each must land the right hold state and route to the right resolver.
- Requisition approval: an approver chain must be enforced by budget and spend category, with no bypass path.
- GR/IR (goods receipt / invoice receipt) reconciliation: unmatched receipts and unmatched invoices must each surface in a clean exception report.
- VAT/tax: line tax must be derived from the right tax code and jurisdiction on both invoice and payment.
- Integration: an invoice arriving via supplier portal vs EDI must behave identically.
