## What this system is

HR systems cover the second-most-sensitive master data a company owns — **people**. The core is the HRIS/HCM: the authoritative employee record (hire date, org, job, comp, location). Around it orbit payroll, benefits, time & attendance, recruiting (ATS), and learning. Below it sits the integration that matters most operationally: **every HR event fanning out to identity, access, and finance**.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  H[Hire event] --> R[HRIS - employee record]
  R --> P[Payroll]
  R --> B[Benefits + time]
  R --> I[Identity provisioning]
  R --> F[Finance - cost center postings]
```

## The hire-to-retire chain

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  J[Job requisition] --> Recruit[Recruiting - ATS]
  Recruit --> Offer[Hire - HRIS record]
  Offer --> Provision[Provision identity + accounts]
  Provision --> Move[Transfers and changes over time]
  Move --> Term[Termination / offboarding]
  Term --> Deprovision[Deprovision identity + access]
  Term --> Pay[Final pay + compliance records]
```

The chain is an **event bus with states**: the terminal states matter as much as the happy path. A leaver whose identity is never deprovisioned is the classic insider-threat hole. This guide deliberately pairs with [joiner-mover-leaver](../joiner-mover-leaver) — that page owns the access half.

## The HR systems landscape

| System | Owns | Master data | Common failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HRIS / HCM | Authoritative employee record, org, jobs | Name, ID, hire/term dates, org, comp, location | Corrections never propagate downstream |
| Payroll | Pay runs, taxes, banking | Pay rates, bank details, tax jurisdiction | Wrong file released on cutoff day |
| ATS (talent) | Candidates, requisitions, interviews | Candidate + applicant trackers | Duplicate candidate records |
| Benefits / time | Enrollment, leave, attendance | Plan elections, time balances | Missed yearly open-enrollment sync |
| Learning / performance | Courses, goals, reviews | Course IDs, reviewer links | Orphaned learning plans |

## The field of derivatives

Almost every downstream system is a **derived copy** of the HRIS record: identity systems, single sign-on, directories, finance cost-center postings, benefits carriers, learning platforms. The HRIS is the source of truth; everything else subscribes. Two integration truths follow:

- **Identity provisioning is correctness, not convenience.** Provisioners (SCIM) turn HR events into directory/SSO/application access mechanically. Verifiable either way: the new hire can sign in before the first day, or the mystery of why they can't is traced here first.
- **Payroll is the highest-consequence batch.** A single run touches thousands of bank movements and statutory filings. Nobody "fixes it in the next release" without regulatory impact.

## The hire → first paycheck path

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  Hire["Hire event"] -->|create| HRIS["HRIS record"]
  HRIS -->|SCIM provision| ID["Identity + directory"]
  ID -->|first login| SSO["Single sign-on"]
  HRIS -->|payroll setup| Pay["Payroll setup"]
  Pay -->|run payroll| Bank["Bank + compliance"]
  SSO -->|access| Ready["Productive employee"]
  Bank -->|pay| Ready
```

## Tester checklist

- Joiner: with only an HR event dated in the future, confirm the identity record exists and is *disabled* until the effective date, and the correct SSO/app grants activate on it.
- Mover: a transfer changing cost center must post to finance with the new org, not silently keep the old one.
- Leaver: termination date passes → accounts deprovisioned, sessions revoked, licenses reclaimed, payroll closed out.
- Payroll cutoff: a change arriving after the payroll batch snapshot must NOT mutate that already-closed run.
- Data quality: a corrected SSN/name in HRIS must flow to every derivative with no stale duplicate.
