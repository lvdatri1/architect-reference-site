## What this system is

A CRM is the **front office**: customer-facing teams start and transact here. It owns the customer's whole journey — marketing touch, lead, opportunity, quote, order, case, and renewal — before (and after) the ERP ever sees a transaction. Where the ERP is the *system of record for money*, the CRM is the *system of record for relationships*.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  Lead[Lead] --> Qual[Qualify] --> Opp[Opportunity] --> Quote[Quote] --> Win[Closed won] --> Order[Order into ERP] --> Service[Service + renewals]
```

## The core objects

| Object | Owner | State it tracks | ERP handoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead / prospect | Marketing + sales | New → qualified | Converted to account+contact |
| Account / contact | Sales ops | Hierarchy, territory | Customer master |
| Opportunity | Sales | Stage, amount, probability | None until won |
| Quote | Sales | Draft → accepted | Order / contract |
| Order | Sales or ERP | Confirmed → shipped | The real handoff |
| Case / ticket | Service | Open → resolved | Field-service or warranty |

The dirty secret of CRM architecture: **a CRM without an ERP handoff is a list of wishes**. The quote-to-order moment is where the relationship system must hand an unambiguous, priced, legal order to the money system — and that handoff is most of the integration work.

## Sales pipeline, the funnel

```mermaid
flowchart TB
  L[Leads in] --> Q1[Qualified - meetings happen]
  Q1 --> P[Proposal / quote]
  P --> W{Win or lose}
  W -->|win| O[Closed won - handoff to ERP]
  W -->|lose| L2[Closed lost - keep for future]
  O --> S[Service: cases, renewals, upsell]
```

Funnel analytics is where the CRM earns its keep: **conversion rates between stages** tell you where the funnel leaks. The stages are configuration; the *metrics* are the system.

## The handoff, done right

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  Lead["Lead"] -->|qualify| Opp["Opportunity"]
  Opp -->|configure - price| Quote["Quote (CPQ)"]
  Quote -->|accepted| Won["Closed won"]
  Won -->|order + contract| ERP["ERP order"]
  ERP -->|fulfill + onboard| Serv["Service + renewal"]
```

## Tester checklist

- Quote-to-order: a quote accepted in CRM must create exactly one order in the ERP with matching line items, prices, and currency — no double entry.
- Field-level match: the customer the CRM is "in love with" must be the same customer the ERP bills (account mapping, not a name string).
- Funnel truth: stages must match the sales process, and a win/lose reason must be enforced — not free text.
- Role-scoped data: sales reps see only their territory, and the service desk sees the full history without editing it.
